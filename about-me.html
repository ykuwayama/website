About Me

I am the Director of the Economic Analysis Hub at the Environmental Defense Fund (EDF). Previously, I was Associate Professor in the School of Public Policy at the University of Maryland, Baltimore County, and a Fellow at Resources for the Future (RFF).

I am a member of the Water Science and Technology Board (WSTB) at the National Academies and also serve on the Boards of Directors of the Association of Environmental and Resource Economists (AERE) and the Northeastern Agricultural and Resource Economics Association (NAREA).

I was born in Toronto, Canada, and raised in Santiago, Chile, where I lived until I graduated high school. When I'm not working, I enjoy hiking, running, cooking, and spending time with my family.
